As project financiers, we pride ourselves in using creative ways to provide the funding you require during this financial crunch and recessions, we are always here to bail you out financially.

Alliance Capital Investment has been in the International Project Funding business since the year 2000, during that time our ability to evolve in these ever changing economic times has led to our success. We have continued while many financial institutions have failed.

We provide Venture Capital Funding all over the world, in numerous currencies. We have given life to projects that have gone on to create both wealth for their owners and secure jobs for workers in many countries.

Alliance Capital Investment has the privilege of working with numerous national and international brokers, attorneys, banks, financial institutions, insurance companies as well as many large and small Project Owners.

As we are a Worldwide Project Investment Group with a Private Fund and have our own private investor capital, we have the resources to fund projects globally. The minimum lending requirement is 1 Million USD/EURO/GBP to 1 Billion USD/EURO/GBP. We have the capability to fund upwards of 1 Billion USD/EURO/GBP by using our syndicated partners. Alliance Capital Investment is able to offer low interest rates and if necessary a grace free period of repayment until a project has adequate cash flow. International Project Funding and Private Capital for Commercial Projects Worldwide

Alliance Capital Investment has been in the International Project Funding business since the year 2000, during that time our ability to evolve in these ever changing economic times has led to our success. We have continued while many financial institutions have failed. The firm holds the 2022 cfi.co award for Best International Project Finance Team, and is registered with Standard & Poor’s, S&P Capital IQ and Dun & Bradstreet.

Alliance Capital Investment provides venture capital funding worldwide, in USD, EURO and GBP. Funded projects include multifamily real estate and orchard farm expansion in Kenya, renewable energy production in Indonesia, textile factory expansion in Rwanda, agriculture expansion in Tanzania, hotel construction in Ghana and a rideshare start-up in Nigeria — each creating operating businesses and local employment.

Alliance Capital Investment works with brokers, attorneys, banks, financial institutions and insurance companies, and funds projects for owners and sponsors at every scale, from a $6 million rideshare start-up to a $225 million renewable energy programme. Brokers presenting client applications are welcome: two of the three client testimonials on this page are from brokers, one of whom has worked with the firm for ten years.

Alliance Capital Investment deploys its own private investor capital. As a worldwide project investment group operating a private fund, the firm funds projects globally without depending on third-party lending lines.

The minimum lending requirement for international project funding is 1 Million USD/EURO/GBP, and facilities run to 1 Billion USD/EURO/GBP. Transactions above that range are funded through syndicated partners.

Our 100% Project Funding program combines 60% private lending with 40% private equity in the project, so no sponsor equity contribution is required. Interest rates are low, and a grace period on repayment is available until the project generates adequate cash flow.

Under current international financial regulations, all costs arising after acceptance of the Loan Investment Commitment Contract are covered by the applicant. This requirement applies to every transaction and is disclosed before any contract is signed.

Our Services
Alliance Capital Investment operates through six specialized service divisions, each addressing a distinct capital need: project finance, venture capital and JV funding, credit enhancement, bridge loans, green funding for ESG-aligned projects, and commercial and indemnity insurance integration.

Green Funding

Alliance Capital Investment provides green funding for ESG-aligned projects, including renewable energy production in Indonesia and orchard farm expansion in Kenya. Feasibility validation, financial structuring and ESG governance oversight are set out on the Green Funding page.

Bridge Loans & International Investment Loan Programs

Alliance Capital Investment structures bridge loans and international investment loan programs for time-sensitive closings, development transitions and refinancing cycles. Because the capital is our own, a funding decision does not wait on a third-party credit committee.

Worldwide Commercial Project Finance

Alliance Capital Investment arranges worldwide commercial project finance covering capital stack optimization, credit enhancement and structured documentation alignment. Applicants declined by banks are eligible: qualification rests on financial feasibility, governance readiness and risk mitigation, not on an existing banking relationship. Venture Capital & JV Funding Programs

Venture capital and JV funding for operating businesses is growth-stage capital provided as a structured combination of private lending and private equity, arranged for companies that already have revenue and need expansion capital rather than seed funding. Qualification rests on the project’s cash-flow profile, not on the sponsor’s balance sheet alone. The platform includes:

  • Venture capital compliance solutions
  • Venture fund management coordination
  • JV Venture Capital Project Funding Program
  • Venture capital advisory services for US and international markets

AAY supports disciplined venture capital investment fund strategies designed for transparency and scalability.

Commercial & Indemnity Insurance Integration

Alliance Capital Investment integrates commercial insurance and indemnity insurance directly into the funding structure, so contractual and operational exposure is covered within the same transaction rather than arranged separately by the sponsor.

Credit Enhancement

Credit enhancement in structured finance is the set of measures that reduce a project’s perceived risk so it can access funding on better terms. Alliance Capital Investment applies it within the same facility, rather than sourcing it from a separate provider, reinforcing project bankability and investor positioning.

Investor Services & Reporting Standards

Investment fund services at Alliance Capital Investment cover capital structuring, compliance coordination, governance oversight and the reporting systems that keep pooled capital accountable. The firm has provided them since 2000 for clients in more than 42 countries, and applies the same four controls to every mandate:

  • Transparent reporting protocols
  • Documentation control
  • Compliance monitoring
  • Structured communication frameworks

Our approach strengthens clarity between sponsors, managers, and capital providers.
