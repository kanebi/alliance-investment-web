Worldwide Venture Capital

Alliance Capital Investment Company is an investment firm building the bridge between visionary projects and global capital since 2000. It operates as a subsidiary of the Middle East Investment Company, which is headquartered in the United Arab Emirates (UAE).

We will be pleased to assist you no matter what your funding requirements are. If you have been turned down by banks or financial institutions, we can help. As project financiers, we pride ourselves in using creative ways to provide the funding you require during this financial crunch and recessions, we are always here to bail you out financially.

Alliance Capital Investment has been in the International Project Funding business since the year 2000, during that time our ability to evolve in these ever changing economic times has led to our success. We have continued while many financial institutions have failed.

We provide Venture Capital Funding all over the world, in numerous currencies. We have given life to projects that have gone on to create both wealth for their owners and secure jobs for workers in many countries.

Alliance Capital Investment has the privilege of working with numerous national and international brokers, attorneys, banks, financial institutions, insurance companies as well as many large and small Project Owners.

As we are a Worldwide Project Investment Group with a Private Fund and have our own private investor capital, we have the resources to fund projects globally. The minimum lending requirement is 1 Million USD/EURO/GBP to 1 Billion USD/EURO/GBP. We have the capability to fund upwards of 1 Billion USD/EURO/GBP by using our syndicated partners. Alliance Capital Investment is able to offer low interest rates and if necessary a grace free period of repayment until a project has adequate cash flow. This is achieved by using our unique 100% Project Funding program, which consists of 60% private lending and 40% private equity in the project.

In order to comply with the new worldwide financial regulations, it is now a requirement for all costs, upon acceptance of the Loan Investment Commitment Contract, to be covered by the applicant.

Our Services

Rather than offering generic funding, we operate through specialized service divisions designed to address distinct capital needs.

Green Funding

Sustainable development requires responsible capital. Our Green Funding solutions support environmentally aligned investment projects while maintaining structured governance controls. We focus on feasibility validation, financial structuring, and ESG-aligned oversight.

Bridge Loans & International Investment Loan Programs

Liquidity gaps can delay high-value transactions. Our Bridge Loans are structured to support time-sensitive closings, development transitions, and refinancing cycles. Each facility is evaluated through risk assessment and compliance review to maintain financial stability.

Worldwide Commercial Project Finance

As one of the internationally focused project finance companies supporting cross-border initiatives, we coordinate structured funding for commercial developments and large-scale expansion projects. Our worldwide commercial project finance services emphasize capital stack optimization, credit enhancement, and structured documentation alignment.

Real Estate

Invest in the real economy

Alliance Capital Investment is one of the 50 largest real estate private equity investors in the world by total annual deployment — deploying more than $1 billion of capital annually in 2021 and 2022.

Our portfolio is largely composed of 20,000+ well-located residential units and eCommerce-centric industrial assets.

Venture Capital & JV Funding Programs

Growth-stage companies and sponsors require structured capital with operational oversight. Our venture capital service platform includes:

  • Venture capital compliance solutions
  • Venture fund management coordination
  • JV Venture Capital Project Funding Program
  • Venture capital advisory services for US and international markets

We support disciplined venture capital investment fund strategies designed for transparency and scalability.

Commercial & Indemnity Insurance Integration

Risk protection strengthens institutional confidence. Our Commercial Insurance and Indemnity Insurance services integrate into funding structures to mitigate contractual and operational exposure.

Credit Enhancement

Competitive funding often depends on financial credibility. Through structured Credit Enhancement strategies, we help reinforce project bankability and investor positioning.

FAQs

What are investment fund services?

Investment fund services include capital structuring, compliance coordination, governance oversight, and reporting systems that ensure responsible management of pooled capital.

How does venture capital fund administration support investors?

Venture capital fund administration improves transparency, strengthens documentation controls, and aligns reporting practices with regulatory expectations.

What qualifies a project for worldwide commercial project finance?

Projects must demonstrate financial feasibility, governance readiness, structured documentation, and risk mitigation measures before funding coordination.

What is credit enhancement in structured finance?

Credit enhancement strengthens financial positioning by reducing perceived risk and improving institutional confidence in funding structures.

How are regulatory and compliance risks managed?

Risks are managed through structured due diligence, documentation review, compliance alignment, and transparent stakeholder reporting.
